What operations actually means

Reality check

The invisible backbone

In wealth management, ops is where the actual magic happens. Trade settlements, compliance workflows, client reporting, system integrations, data integrity. If one thing breaks, everyone panics. Jessica's job is to make sure nobody panics.

Translation

Professional chaos absorber

Portfolio managers say "buy". Research says "sell". Compliance says "wait". The client says "why hasn't this happened yet?" Operations is the person in the middle making all of these people feel heard while quietly fixing everything behind the scenes.

The truth

Anchor would not function without ops

This is not flattery, it's just structural reality. You can have the best investment thesis in Africa, but if your operations can't execute, reconcile, report, and comply — you're just a person with opinions and a Bloomberg terminal.
