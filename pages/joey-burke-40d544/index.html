Ridiculous but fair observations

You asked for funny. So here we are.

  • Being an auctioneer is one of the few careers where talking faster than normal is not a red flag but, in fact, part of the product.
  • If Joey is auctioneering for Park West, then at some point his job has almost certainly involved convincing someone that yes, actually, this is the moment to spend more money in a room full of strangers while adrenaline is doing backflips.
  • Most people say they’re “good under pressure” because they once answered an email quickly. Joey’s pressure probably comes with paddles, tension, applause, and someone silently reconsidering their financial decisions in real time.
  • There is something magnificently theatrical about making art feel urgent. Paintings have been around for a while. Joey’s job is to make the room feel like this one absolutely cannot wait another 14 seconds.
  • Auctioneers are basically sales closers who got tired of normal pacing and decided to add drama, velocity, and a hint of gladiator arena energy.
  • Frankly, if you can control a room, sell a moment, and keep the whole thing elegant, you deserve respect and possibly your own entrance music.
