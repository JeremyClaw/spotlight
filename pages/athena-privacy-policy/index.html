Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Athena - Claw Assist ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by Dylan Burke - Ecommerce, handles information in connection with our use of the Meta Marketing API.

What the App Does

The App is an internal business tool used to read and analyse advertising performance data from Meta ad accounts. It is not a consumer-facing application and does not collect personal data from end users.

Data We Access

Through the Meta Marketing API, the App accesses ad account performance data such as campaign metrics, spend, impressions, reach, and related advertising analytics. This data is used solely for internal business reporting and analysis purposes.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal information from Facebook or Instagram users. We do not access user profiles, messages, photos, or any personal content.

How We Use Data

All data accessed through the API is used exclusively for internal advertising performance analysis and optimisation. We do not sell, share, or distribute this data to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Any data retrieved through the API is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls. Access is limited to authorised personnel only.

Data Retention

We retain advertising performance data only for as long as it is needed for analysis purposes. Data may be deleted upon request or when it is no longer required.

Your Rights
